Ghats & Bathing Points

Explore the sacred ghats along the Godavari River where millions of devotees take the holy dip.

The Godavari Pushkaralu spans multiple states — Maharashtra, Telangana, and Andhra Pradesh — with dozens of ghats open to devotees during the festival.

🔴 Andhra Pradesh

Rajamahendravaram (Rajahmundry)

📍 East Godavari District, Andhra Pradesh

Rajahmundry is the principal venue of the Godavari Pushkaralu in Andhra Pradesh. It hosts the largest concentration of ghats including the famous Pushkar Ghat, Godavari Ghat, and Ardhanareeswara Ghat. The Kotipalli and Talupulamma temples are major pilgrimage spots here.

🚿 Bathing Facilities 🚑 Medical Camp 🏕️ Tent Shelters 🚌 Bus Services 🚂 Rail Access ♿ Accessibility

Dowleswaram

📍 Near Rajahmundry, Andhra Pradesh

Located near the Dowleswaram barrage, this ghat is one of the busiest during Pushkaralu. The Uttara Vahini (northward-flowing) point of the Godavari here is considered especially sacred and is a top priority for devotees seeking maximum spiritual merit.

🚿 Bathing Facilities 🚑 Medical Camp 🅿️ Parking 🛡️ Security

Kovvur

📍 West Godavari District, Andhra Pradesh

Kovvur sits on the opposite bank of Rajahmundry and serves as an important ghat for devotees arriving from the West Godavari district. Regular ferry services connect Kovvur and Rajahmundry during the festival.

🚿 Bathing Facilities ⛵ Ferry Services 🚑 Medical Camp

🟠 Telangana

Bhadrachalam

📍 Bhadradri Kothagudem District, Telangana

Home to the revered Bhadrachalam temple dedicated to Lord Rama, Bhadrachalam is one of the holiest towns on the Godavari. The town is especially important for Vaishnava devotees. Lord Rama is said to have crossed the Godavari here during his search for Sita.

🛕 Temple 🚿 Bathing Facilities 🏨 Accommodation 🚑 Medical Camp

Basar

📍 Nirmal District, Telangana

Basar houses the famous Sri Gnana Saraswati Temple, one of only two Saraswati temples in India and the only one on the banks of the Godavari. Ayudha Puja and Aksharabhyasam (initiation of learning) ceremonies are conducted here in large numbers.

🛕 Saraswati Temple 🚿 Bathing Facilities 📚 Aksharabhyasam 🚌 Bus Services

Dharmapuri

📍 Jagtial District, Telangana

A significant Pushkar ghat in Telangana, Dharmapuri is known for the Sri Narasimha Swamy temple. The ghat on the Godavari here attracts tens of thousands of devotees during the festival.

🛕 Temple 🚿 Bathing Facilities 🅿️ Parking

🟡 Maharashtra (Origin — Nashik / Trimbakeshwar)

Trimbakeshwar

📍 Nashik District, Maharashtra

Trimbakeshwar is the origin (source) of the Godavari River and home to one of the 12 Jyotirlinga shrines of Lord Shiva. The Kund (sacred tank) here is where the Godavari emerges from the earth. It is a non-negotiable pilgrimage stop for devout Hindus.

🛕 Jyotirlinga Temple 🌊 Sacred Kund 🏨 Accommodation 🚌 Bus Services

Nashik (Panchavati)

📍 Nashik, Maharashtra

Nashik's Panchavati area on the Godavari banks is where Lord Rama, Sita, and Lakshmana spent time during their exile. Ram Kund, a sacred bathing ghat, is the focal point of Pushkar activities here and is believed to grant moksha to those who bathe in it.

🛕 Ram Kund 🚿 Bathing Ghats 🎭 Cultural Programs 🚂 Rail Access

⚠️ Safety Guidelines at Ghats

  • Always follow the directions of police and security personnel.
  • Avoid overcrowded areas — use alternate ghats if available.
  • Children and elderly should not enter deep water areas.
  • Wear life jackets if taking a boat ride.
  • Stay hydrated and avoid sun exposure in peak afternoon hours.
  • Keep emergency helpline numbers saved on your phone.
  • Do not litter — help keep the Godavari clean and sacred.
