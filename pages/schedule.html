Festival Schedule

12 sacred days of Godavari Pushkaralu — auspicious timings, rituals, and cultural programs.

Exact dates will be officially announced based on Jupiter's transit into Simha Rashi. The schedule below reflects the traditional structure of the 12-day festival.

12-Day Festival Schedule

Day Name Date (2027) Key Rituals Special
1 Adi Pushkaram TBA Sacred bath, Pushkar Snanam, Puja Most Auspicious
2 Dwitiya Pushkaram TBA Pitru Tarpanam, Danam
3 Tritiya Pushkaram TBA Mahalakshmi Puja, Homa
4 Chaturthi Pushkaram TBA Vinayaka Puja, Japa
5 Panchami Pushkaram TBA Navagraha Puja, Abhishekam
6 Shashthi Pushkaram TBA Saraswati Puja, Vedic recitation
7 Saptami Pushkaram TBA Surya Puja, lamp offerings
8 Ashtami Pushkaram TBA Durga Puja, Homa
9 Navami Pushkaram TBA Rama Puja, Ramayana recital
10 Dashami Pushkaram TBA Pitru Shraddham, deep daan
11 Ekadashi Pushkaram TBA Vishnu Puja, fasting, Bhajans
12 Anthya Pushkaram TBA Final sacred bath, closing Aarti Last Day

Daily Auspicious Timings

Activity Time Notes
Brahma Muhurtham (Early Bath) 4:00 AM – 5:30 AM Most auspicious time for holy dip
Morning Puja 6:00 AM – 8:00 AM Prayers at river ghats
Pitru Tarpanam 10:00 AM – 12:00 PM Water offerings to ancestors
Afternoon Rituals 1:00 PM – 3:00 PM Homam, Danam
Evening Aarti 6:30 PM – 7:30 PM River Aarti with lamps
Cultural Programs 7:30 PM – 10:00 PM Music, dance, spiritual discourses

Cultural Programs

🎵

Carnatic Music Concerts

Classical Carnatic vocal and instrumental performances by noted artists from across South India.

💃

Classical Dance

Kuchipudi, Bharatanatyam and other classical dance recitals on open-air stages near the ghats.

📖

Harikatha & Discourses

Spiritual storytelling (Harikatha), Vedic lectures, and discourses by renowned scholars.

🪘

Folk Arts

Traditional folk performances including Kolattam, Burrakatha, and Veedhi Natakam.

Full program details will be announced closer to the festival dates.
