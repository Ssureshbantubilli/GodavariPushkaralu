Festival Highlights

🙏

Sacred Bathing

Take a holy dip in the Godavari at auspicious ghats and cleanse the soul during the 12-day festival.

🛕

Temple Rituals

Experience special sevas, poojas, and abhishekams at ancient temples along the riverbanks.

🎶

Cultural Programs

Enjoy classical music, dance performances, spiritual discourses, and folk arts throughout the festival.

🗺️

Pilgrimage Circuits

Embark on a journey through sacred towns including Rajahmundry, Nashik, Trimbakeshwar, and more.

🌊

River Aarti

Witness the mesmerizing Godavari Aarti performed every evening on the river with thousands of lamps.

📜

Vedic Chants

Listen to ancient Vedic hymns and mantras recited by learned priests during special rituals.
